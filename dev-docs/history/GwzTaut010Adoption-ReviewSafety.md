## 1. Findings

### [P3-1] The release-provenance refusal binds the module to whatever `taut-proto` metadata comes first on `sys.path`, so a checkout that carries its own dist-info passes, and no content pin remains

**Location.** `gwz-transport/scripts/regen.py:24` (`released_taut`) and `:37` (`verify_taut_modules`); `gwz-core/protocol/candidate/candidate-regenerator.py:61` (`_released_taut`) and `:98` (`_check_taut_origins`); `gwz-core/tests/transport_consumer/protocol/regen.py:86` (`_released_taut`), `:100`, `:111`. The claims: `gwz-transport/README.md:307` ("refusing a `taut` that shadows it from a checkout or `PYTHONPATH`"), `gwz-core/tests/transport_consumer/README.md:33–34` ("a `taut` found ahead of it on `sys.path` or `PYTHONPATH`, such as the workspace's `taut/src`, is refused"), the docstrings at `regen.py:5` and `candidate-regenerator.py:14`, and the brief §2 ("so a checkout on `sys.path` or `PYTHONPATH` can't shadow it") and §3.5.

**Violated invariant.** "Generation imports taut only from the installed release the pin names." What the code enforces is weaker: `importlib.metadata.distribution("taut-proto")` returns the first matching metadata directory on `sys.path` (PYTHONPATH entries precede site-packages), its `.version` is compared as a string, and every loaded `taut.*` module's `__file__` must lie under `locate_file("taut")` of that same entry. Anything that ships package and metadata together ahead of site-packages satisfies all three checks.

**Reproduction (run here, writes only under `mktemp -d`).** `T=$(mktemp -d); mkdir $T/taut_proto-0.10.0.dist-info; printf 'Metadata-Version: 2.1\nName: taut-proto\nVersion: 0.10.0\n' > $T/taut_proto-0.10.0.dist-info/METADATA; cp -R taut/src/taut $T/taut; echo '# LOCAL MODIFICATION' >> $T/taut/gen/scaffold.py`; then with `PYTHONPATH=$T`: `gwz-transport/scripts/regen.py --check` printed "4 generated artifacts verified" (exit 0); the candidate regenerator printed "placement candidate regeneration verified" (exit 0); the consumer regen printed "consumer regen verified" (exit 0). The probe confirmed the checks saw `dist path: $T/taut_proto-0.10.0.dist-info`, `version: 0.10.0`, `taut.__file__: $T/taut/__init__.py`. Control: the same copy with the dist-info removed was refused by all three ("Imported taut._version is not the installed taut-proto release"), which is the only case the new shadow tests (`test_a_taut_that_shadows_the_release_is_refused`, staging a package without metadata) exercise.

**Impact.** A modified or unreleased generator can regenerate, and pass `--check`, while every manifest says 0.10.0. The removed checkout mode pinned content (`taut-source-sha256` for 14 files, `taut-extension-sha256`, a clean-tree check); nothing replaces that, so the only remaining backstop is a `--check` run under a fresh PyPI install. That backstop exists for gwz-transport (`contracts.yml`) but not for the candidate and consumer generators: `gwz-core/scripts/run_tests.py` and no workflow runs `candidate-regenerator.py` or `transport_consumer/protocol/regen.py` (the candidate CI job is deferred by the brief §4). For those two, the local refusal the text presents as the guard is the whole guard. Bounded to developer-local regeneration of candidate-only and test-consumer artifacts, and this step's own artifacts are independently proven (golden vectors unchanged; the evidence's `--check` ran against the PyPI wheel in `protocol/.regen-venv` and gwz-py's `.venv`, which I confirmed is a plain wheel install with no `.pth` pointing at a checkout), so P3, not P2.

**Required correction.** Any one of: (a) require the distribution's metadata directory to lie under the interpreter's site directories (`site.getsitepackages()`/`sysconfig.get_paths()["purelib"]`, plus the user site) before trusting its version and location; (b) restore a content pin — record the SHA-256 of the installed `taut` package files (or the wheel's `RECORD`) in the three generator manifests and verify before `import taut`, the role the removed digests played; and in all cases (c) reword the two READMEs, the two docstrings and the brief to state what is enforced.
