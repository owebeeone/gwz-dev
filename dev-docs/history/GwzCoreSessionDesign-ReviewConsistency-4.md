### [P3-34] Four statements say a session-path child receives only the environment snapshot, while §5.8 now specifies a child that receives the snapshot with two variables removed and one added
- **Location:** O9 line 82 ("The session's child processes get only its environment snapshot"); §5.6 line 295 ("spawned with `env_clear()` plus the snapshot, as the `gh` helper already is"); §5.7 row line 346 ("spawned with `env_clear()` plus the snapshot (§5.8)"); GWZDesign line 13 ("Its child processes receive only the session's environment snapshot"); GWZRequirements' O9 bullet ("Session-path child processes MUST receive only the session's environment snapshot") — versus §5.8 line 360 ("The spawn has `env_clear()` plus the snapshot without `GIT_ASKPASS` and `SSH_ASKPASS`, plus `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT=0`"). Source: `https_auth.rs` 324–326 shows the `gh` helper, the model the contract cites, already adds `GH_PROMPT_DISABLED=1` and `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT=0`.
- **Violated invariant:** the paired paragraphs make the same claims as the contract, and a MUST in GWZRequirements is met by the mechanism the contract prescribes. gwz-core's `AGENTS.md` makes GWZRequirements the baseline behaviour may not exceed.
- **Reproduction:** an implementer follows §5.8 and spawns `git credential fill` with `GIT_TERMINAL_PROMPT=0`; a reviewer checking against GWZRequirements finds a session-path child receiving a value that is not in the snapshot and flags a MUST violation; a test written to O9's sentence ("only its environment snapshot", asserting no extra variables) fails for this spawn and for the existing `gh` spawn alike. Before revision 4 the additions existed only in code; revision 4 wrote them into the contract beside the unqualified rule.
- **Impact:** the rule and its own mechanism disagree in the text; the disagreement is new to revision 4 and lands in the baseline document.
- **Required correction:** qualify the four statements in one clause, for example: "nothing from the live environment beyond the snapshot; prompt hooks may be removed and prompt-disabling settings added, as §5.8 states for `git credential fill` and the `gh` helper already does"; leave §5.8 as it is.
- **Closure test:** §15.8's non-UTF-8 byte test (presence) plus a new assertion that a session-path child's environment contains no variable from the live process environment that is absent from the snapshot; the recorder test at §15.8 line 614 for the removed hooks.
- **Classification:** bounded text correction; not architectural.
