## 1 Findings

### P2 — The shadow phase cannot validate a legal continuation after late Opened

**Location:** `GwzTransportV3ReorderingDesign.md:28,44`, read with its within-stream causal rule at `:11,36`.

**Root cause:** A tombstone saved in Opening may advance a shadow phase “solely to validate an ensuing Closed after Opened.” The rule does not admit the ordinary stream frames that can intervene. Conversely, Closed requires a prior initiator Close; an initiator that retired while still Opening could not have sent that Close without violating the draft’s Opened prerequisite.

**Sequence:** Requests A and B share one binding. A’s Open is admitted. The endpoint queues Opened, then an established Window or Data frame, and later a failure terminal. Before Opened reaches the initiator, A reaches its local cleanup deadline and its Opening route is retired; B remains active. The late Opened is contained and advances A’s shadow phase. The next correctly correlated Window or Data was generated by the established endpoint and remains in flight. Under the saved Opening phase it is not legal, while the specified shadow phase exists only for an ensuing Closed. Rejecting it as Protocol fails B’s healthy binding. A test that substitutes Opened followed immediately by Closed cannot prove this case: the endpoint’s Closed requires an earlier initiator Close, which A could not send before receiving Opened.

**Impact:** Normal delivery and timeout timing can turn A’s contained cleanup into a binding-wide failure for B. The proposed closure test can also pass only by permitting a transition that the stream handshake does not authorize.

**Correction:** Define the shadow state after late Opened as a bounded, non-authoritative established-stream validation state. Specify which correctly correlated Window, Data, control and terminal frames it contains, with their ordering and causal prerequisites; none may change A’s outcome, facts or lease ownership. Admit Closed only if a valid prior Close was actually sent. Replace the Opened-then-Closed proof case with a legal late-Opened continuation, including intervening traffic and a legal terminal.

**Closure test:** Admit A and B; retire A locally while Opening with Opened still in flight. Deliver A’s Opened, an already generated Window or Data frame, and a legal failure terminal under per-key ordering. Assert A stays retired, B continues, and no facts or lease change. Inject an unsolicited Closed without a prior Close and verify Protocol. Separately test Closed after a genuinely established stream sent Close before local retirement.
