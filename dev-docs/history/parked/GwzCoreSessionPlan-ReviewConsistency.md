### [P3-1] Ordinary-path marking stops at TR1.4a's own steps although §6(b) and TR1.4a's list say TR1.4a marks each one
- **Location.** §3.0 "Ordinary-path steps", last bullet (line 129): "Revision 1 marks CS1.1, CS2.5, CS2.10, CS3.4, CS3.9, CS6.2, CS6.3 and CS6.4. TR1.4b marks the steps it revises". CS4.7 (454) and CS4.8 (460) carry no marker.

The defect is that the plan's ordinary-path ledger, which the Safety axis attacks by §7's own list ("an ordinary-path step left unmarked"), is incomplete by construction, and the transport plan's "each one" is not met by revision 1.
- **Remedy.** Mark CS4.7 and CS4.8 in revision 1 with R8's reasons; keep §3.0's sentence only for steps TR1.4b adds (reuse and server steps). CS3.10 is settled under P3-2.
- **Closure test.** Every step whose text or R8 names a change for an existing gwz, gwz-py or crate user carries `**Ordinary path:**`; §3.0's list equals the set of marked steps.

- **Reproduction.** Today `transport_capabilities` is answered by `gwz-core/src/protocol/transport_capabilities.rs::handle`, reached by gwz-py's native dispatch (`native/src/dispatch/read.rs:32`) until CS4.7; gwz-cli exposes no such route.
